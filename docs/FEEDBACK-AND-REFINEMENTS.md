# Reported interaction feedback and refinement contract

Recorded 13 September 2026 from an owner phone review of the three-toy preview. The exact phone model, OS, build label, motion setting and audio settings were not recorded. These are product observations and design requests, not independently reproduced defects or evidence of general player outcomes. The raw report and private play observations remain in ignored local storage.

The checked v0.1.1 adds Penguin Bounce. It does **not** resolve the reported interaction limitations of the other three toys. Keep this boundary visible in the delivery record, roadmap and backlog.

| Area | Retain | Reported shortcoming | Next bounded acceptance target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Squishy Friend | Current original character design, raster finish and local deformation quality. | The available stretch is too small and the response lacks expressive variation. | Prioritize a visibly larger range of local pulls on phone/tablet without mesh inversion, loss of four-contact ownership or unreachable geometry. Explore separate original face/eye/body layers with independent local response; evaluate the visual result before committing to that technique. Whole-image scaling is insufficient. |
| Touch response | Predictable direct cause and effect, immediate pause/exit and silent access. | The friend has little interesting response after release. | Evaluate bounded, input-triggered spring/rebound or small character movement in Playful mode. Gentle/system reduced motion must retain a quiet coherent response. No idle attraction loop, off-screen escape, obligatory wait, or unrelated fullscreen stimulation. |
| Bubble Pond | Original iridescent bubble appearance. | Repeated returns to identical positions feel repetitive; a differently colored bubble reads as inconsistent; no sound was heard. | Investigate color consistency and introduce safe spatial variation while keeping large separated targets and preventing respawn beneath held contacts. Existing effects are deliberately silent until adult enable; verify the enabled pop path and improve discoverability without auto-enabling audio. Add meaningful visual response so silent play remains complete. |
| Roll & Nest | Direct ball manipulation and recoverability. | The broad green halo and floating shadow feel out of place; the ball/bowl front-lip occlusion appears wrong; placement offers little response. | Reproduce the overlap at multiple bowl depths and orientations, correct draw order/masking, and remove or redesign the halo/shadow while preserving a readable tap-selection state. Then consider a bounded roll/settle response that keeps pickup, generous placement and independent retrieval. |
| Overall presentation | Original cute raster characters and clear controls. | The shell/backgrounds feel too clinical and corporate. | Develop a warmer, more playful toyroom presentation with purposeful original details and tactile controls. Preserve strong contrast, large touch targets, simple navigation, resource caps and reduced motion; avoid merely adding visual clutter. |
| Variety | All existing toys remain available. | Three simple interactions are insufficient variety. | Penguin Bounce supplies the already-approved fourth interaction. More animals, musical gaps, letters/numbers and optional vignettes remain separate backlog items. Refining the existing interactions has priority over adding another toy. |

## Status and authority

This records the requested direction and concrete reproduction work. It does not retroactively claim that v0.1.1 fixes these items or that an implementation technique has been validated. The next refinement patch should select and specify bounded changes from LJ-13 through LJ-17, retaining the original input, privacy, audio, pause and resource requirements. Prefer original layered art where useful; do not copy an existing game's characters or implementation.

Before closing an item, record the changed visible behavior, reproduced defect or comparison, relevant checks, and the actual build. Keep reports of private reactions separate from public engineering evidence. See [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md), [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and [DELIVERY.md](DELIVERY.md).
